About Us

Divine encounters, restoration, and faith

Bumala Miracle Centre is a place of divine encounters, restoration, and empowering lives through the word of faith. We're a family of believers, elders, deacons, and ministry teams committed to worship, discipleship, and serving one another — in church on Sunday and in homes throughout the week.
